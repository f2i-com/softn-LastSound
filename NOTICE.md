# Notices

LAST SOUND: Below the Call is an unofficial, non-canonical fan story inspired by
Iron Lung. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the creators of
Iron Lung, and it uses none of that game's assets, text or recordings. The story,
the capsule and trench geometry, the survey photographs, the interface and the
sound effects are original work made for this game.

The 181 voice lines were generated with OpenAI text-to-speech from the game's own
script and treated per character with ffmpeg. They are synthetic, not human
recordings. Rights to redistribute generated speech follow the terms of the service
they were generated with.

The build tools in `tools/` are copies of scripts from
[f2i-com/softn.com](https://github.com/f2i-com/softn.com) (Apache-2.0), noted at the top
of each file, so that the game can be built and validated without the engine checkout.
